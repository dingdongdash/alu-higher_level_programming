Doctests for the text_indentation function defined in 5-text_indentation.py
===========================================================================

Load the function:

    >>> text_indentation = __import__('5-text_indentation').text_indentation

The last printed line has no new line at its end, so print() is used
after the function when the text does not end with a delimiter.

Two new lines are printed after a dot:

    >>> text_indentation("Hello.")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>
    >>> text_indentation("Hello. World"); print()
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>
    World

Two new lines are printed after a question mark:

    >>> text_indentation("Hello?")
    Hello?
    <BLANKLINE>
    >>> text_indentation("Hello? World"); print()
    Hello?
    <BLANKLINE>
    World

Two new lines are printed after a colon:

    >>> text_indentation("Hello:")
    Hello:
    <BLANKLINE>
    >>> text_indentation("Hello: World"); print()
    Hello:
    <BLANKLINE>
    World

Other punctuation is not a delimiter:

    >>> text_indentation("Hello, world! Fine; ok - yes (no)"); print()

    >>> text_indentation("Hello"); print()

    >>> text_indentation("a b c d"); print()
